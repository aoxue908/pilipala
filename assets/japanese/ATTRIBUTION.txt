PiliPala integration: tokenize full subtitle sentences in normal mode on an
Android worker, retain UTF-16 source offsets, convert readings to hiragana,
merge supported calendar/time expressions, and leave unknown readings blank.
The engine and dictionaries are unmodified. No subtitle text is transmitted.
Reading selection is statistical; ambiguous dates, names and new words are
not guaranteed to be resolved without additional context.
